FAQ

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. StockLedger runs inside Google Sheets and opens as a web app on any browser. On a phone, use "Add to Home screen" and it opens like a regular app.

Where is my data stored?

In your own Google Sheet, in your own Google Drive. We can't see it. You can export it to Excel or CSV anytime.

Does it work for pharmacies and food businesses?

Yes. Mark a product or category as perishable and the app requires an expiration date for every batch you receive. It sells the earliest-expiring stock first and blocks the sale of expired stock.

Can I use a barcode scanner?

Yes. Any USB or Bluetooth scanner that types into a text field works on the Sell, Receive and Damage screens.

What happens if I cancel?

The app stops opening at the end of your billing period, but your spreadsheet and all your data stay yours.

Do I need a Google account?

Yes. A free Gmail account or Google Workspace works. Each staff member signs in with their own Google account.
